Ten story lessons, one a week, each built the same way: a short Spanish passage with the new words in amber, glosses for the hard support words, the English hidden until you want it, a five-blank cloze, and three speaking prompts. Read → do the cloze → say it out loud. The last status is 'can retell it' because that, not the reading, is the goal. — Every skill, grouped by track and levelled 1–5. Click a level tile to filter. Sources, method & export

Ten story lessons written for this board, A1 → A2 in scope: ser/estar/tener, present tense, gustar, directions, shopping, weather, the preterite, near future. Format borrowed from Olga's own Spanish tutor: Spanish first with new words in amber, support glosses, English hidden behind a toggle, a cloze that blanks only the new words, and speaking prompts — because retelling out loud is the actual test. Companion listening: Coffee Break Spanish, Dreaming Spanish; dictionary: SpanishDict.
